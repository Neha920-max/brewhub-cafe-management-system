How Our Cafe Order Flow Works:

  1. Order Placed: Your order is recorded in the system and assigned a unique receipt number.
  2. Confirmed: Our billing staff verifies the order items and queues them for preparation.
  3. Preparing: Our baristas and chefs brew fresh coffee and cook your food.
  4. Ready: Your food is delivered to your table (dine-in) or called for pickup at the counter.
